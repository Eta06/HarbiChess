## Research directions and evidence

[Laya reference research](MERCEK-decision-models-20261003.md) identifies the external
ModernBERT/dynamic-choice model, not the future general decision model's name.
No forced Laya integration or local chess-performance claim. HarbiChess may later
produce provenance-rich state/legal-action/search/outcome data for a separate
decision model or LLM. Legal moves, actual playing strength and explanation
quality need separate tests; imitation alone does not automatically beat its teacher.
AlphaZero, Gumbel/Mctx, Lc0, KataGo, searchless chess and grounded LLM chess sources
inform mechanisms and controlled hypotheses, not an obligatory old roadmap.

Primary priority: quantify representation/search target quality and game-level
learning improvement with frozen controls; avoid another expensive continuation
of failed gates. Conventional residual deepening/pairwise attention are not
presented as original inventions. Paper-level novelty needs reproducible useful
mechanisms, matched compute, multiple independent seeds and stronger external tests.
